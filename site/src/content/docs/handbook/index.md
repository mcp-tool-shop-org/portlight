Portlight is a trade-first maritime strategy game played in the terminal. You start as a captain with a small ship and limited silver. You build a career through route arbitrage, contracts, infrastructure, finance, and reputation.

The TUI (`portlight tui`) is a complete play path. The CLI is the same game with typed commands. They share a save.

## What makes it different

- **Prices react to your trades.** Dump grain at a port and the price crashes.
- **Contracts require proof.** Right goods, right port, tracked provenance.
- **Infrastructure changes timing.** Warehouses, brokers in all five regions, seven licenses.
- **Reputation is four axes.** Trust, heat, regional standing, underworld.
- **The game reads what you built.** Four victory paths score evidence, not a menu pick.
- **Bounty hunt is a hunt.** Accept a live captain, then `bounty hunt` spawns that encounter.

## The world

- **20 ports** across 5 regions
- **18 goods** with scarcity pricing (including pelts and contraband)
- **43 routes** gated by ship class and season
- **9 captain types**
- **5 ship classes**
- **4 pirate factions**, 8 live named captains
- **134 named NPCs**
- **4 victory paths**
